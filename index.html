The problem

Feedback comes in everywhere. Structure doesn't.

Teams collect customer opinions across email, in-app, and support channels, but approvals, escalation, and reporting are still handled by hand — slow, inconsistent, and easy to lose track of.

The solution

One pipeline, fully automated.

Flowtix uses Claude AI to draft tailored survey questions, routes them through manager approval and auto-delegation on n8n, sends individualized links to each customer, and alerts teams by email and WhatsApp with AI-compiled executive summaries — without manual handoffs.
